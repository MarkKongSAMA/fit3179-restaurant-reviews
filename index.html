BEHIND THE STORY

Sources, choices and limits.

The official geography

City of Melbourne, Café, restaurant, bistro seats (2024 census) and CLUE small areas. Both are licensed CC BY 4.0. Indoor and outdoor seating records are combined by property, business name and address; only the café and restaurant industry is included.

Maps use a conic equal-area projection centred on Melbourne. Density areas and equal-sized bins are calculated in UTM zone 55S. The 2026 processing date is not the census year.

The review sample

Tripadvisor restaurant listings, accessed 6 October 2026: 25 featured listings encountered on the Melbourne page plus six selected fine-dining listings. Selection is non-random. Each record retains its original URL; some reader snapshots may be cached.

Only attributed factual metadata are used; no review text or photographs are reproduced. No open licence is asserted for these listings. Historical building matches are available for 27 listings; four locations without a reliable match are withheld from the map.
