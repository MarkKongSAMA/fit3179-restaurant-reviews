CSV format details

Files use UTF-8 encoding and comma-separated columns. Multiple tags are separated by semicolons; missing values are blank. Text beginning with characters such as + or @ may have a protective leading apostrophe so spreadsheet software reads it as text. Original JSON and GeoJSON retain the original values.
